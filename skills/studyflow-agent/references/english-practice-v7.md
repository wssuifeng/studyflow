# English focused practice and speech generation

Discover `content.practice_groups` and `content.speech_generation` through `system.schema`. For new focused practice, require `studyflow.course-package/8` and set `course.learning_mode` to `english`. The [mixed sample](../examples/course-package-v8.json) combines reading, sentence translation, and writing in the same course. Arrange the lesson and exercise lists in the order the learner should see them. StudyFlow 1.8.26 opens the first or last active item directly from the plan and follows that order across question types. Each spelling, word-form, translation, or rewriting item keeps its `/6` `english` object. Add `READING_GROUP`, `LISTENING_GROUP`, or `ESSAY` as separate exercises with their own fields. A standalone specialty course uses only that specialty's exercises. All of these open the English training surface.

The [legacy `/7` sample](../examples/course-package-v7.json) uses the original `general` authoring contract. StudyFlow 1.8.26 opens those courses with listening, reading, or essay items in the focused view and retains the existing frozen round and submissions. Existing `/6` courses continue to work. When the installed executable lacks `/8`, prepare a draft and report the compatibility limit. Reauthor a legacy sample as a separate `/8` draft, using a new `package_id` and the existing adopted audio. Keep its old course and learning history until the learner authorizes a replacement.

A cloze item uses fixed `{"text":"Bring your "}` and `{"blank_id":"b1"}` fragments within `parts`. Its `blanks` list contains `{"id":"b1","accepted":["ticket"]}`. Put blanks in display order and include each once. Check `content.practice_groups.cloze` and its `schema` and `kinds` before authoring a reading blank or a listening phrase. On supported `/8` builds, list the complete answer for each blank, including any allowed alternatives. For `the library`, `THE LIBRARY` matches. A missing word, extra word, or extra punctuation needs its own accepted form. Comparison applies Unicode NFKC, strips outer whitespace, and ignores case. Put sentence punctuation in fixed text and check each full answer against the audio or passage. Legacy `/7` supports listening blanks with single-word answers and reading multiple choice. Use unrelated examples in learner-visible teaching blocks to avoid giving away the scored answers. A group has at most 20 questions. For multiple audio files, make separate groups.

Use `practice.author.preview` with `package`, `lesson_id`, and `exercise_id`. Provide test `answers` only to preview the completed result. When using the desktop, open 工作区 → 题组预览 and select the JSON file. The preview uses temporary answers while the learner’s frozen round keeps its own state. Evaluate the passage, audio, options, and explanations in that preview before publishing the course package. The endpoint covers `READING_GROUP` and `LISTENING_GROUP`. Validate `ESSAY` with `course validate-package --file <package.json>` and check its editing and feedback in the desktop with an authorized test fixture. Use `course quality-check --file <package.json>` for authoring advisories. Older releases may emit `REFERENCE_MISSING` for local listening and reading groups, whose answer and explanation belong inside the group. Keep validation, preview and device or learner checks as distinct evidence.

## Course audio

- In 工作区 → 语音生成, choose the Step Plan preset or a compatible non-streaming Audio Speech endpoint. Enter the credential into the desktop form. The CLI reads profile metadata with `speech.profile.list` or `speech.profile.get` and uses `profile_id` with the selected version.
- For an authorized synthesis, call `speech.job.start` with a UUID `job_id`, `profile_id`, `expected_profile_version`, and `text` or `segments`. A segment has `text` plus optional `voice`. Multiple segments use WAV and the same sampling settings. Supply `instruction` only when the service accepts it.
- Capture the `speech.job.start` exit status and JSON receipt. Poll `speech.job.get` with the same UUID and workspace after successful registration. If polling returns `OBJECT_NOT_FOUND`, check the start receipt, workspace, UUID, profile version and WAV selection first. After a transport interruption, reconcile the original request and provider record before retrying billable synthesis. Use the original UUID and parameters for an authorized retry. Completed segments are reused.
- After试听, call `speech.asset.adopt`. Copy the `audio` object into `LISTENING_GROUP.listening.audio`, then validate the package. The resulting course and its frozen rounds resolve the asset by SHA-256 and format. Workspace backups include the adopted asset.

## Current-sentence captions

When `content.practice_groups.listening_captions` advertises `/8`, place optional `captions` beside `transcript` in `listening`. Use ordered `{"start":0,"end":1.75,"text":"Meet at the library."}` entries in seconds from the beginning of the adopted file. Each cue covers one sentence or spoken turn. Gaps are allowed. Keep cues inside the audio and free of overlaps, with at most 200 cues, at most 1,200 characters per cue, and a combined maximum of 20,000 characters. Keep `transcript` for post-answer review.

When `content.speech_generation.segmented_captions` is available, write one sentence per `segments` entry and synthesize in WAV. For multiple segments, copy `speech.asset.adopt`'s `captions` array to `listening.captions`, alongside the adopted `audio` reference. Its timestamps follow the actual WAV concatenation boundaries. Check the spoken words and timing in desktop preview with the 当前句 toggle. For an existing recording, use reviewed timecodes or leave the field absent. `/7` and untimed `/8` courses can show estimated captions from their transcript. The desktop initially hides captions and plays the adopted file locally. To inspect the preview caption payload, use `practice.author.preview` with `captions: true` in a separate call from `audio: true`.

## Essay feedback

Use `assignment.summary` with `role=agent` and read the selected essay via `assignment.context`. The exercise type is `ESSAY`. Use four sections within `review.write.detail_markdown` for task fulfillment, organization and coherence, vocabulary and expression, and grammar and mechanics. Link concrete issues to exact excerpts in `issues`, and give a next revision action. Submit a new review against the exact `submission_id` of the draft being reviewed. The revision editor creates a child submission and preserves the earlier draft with its own feedback.
